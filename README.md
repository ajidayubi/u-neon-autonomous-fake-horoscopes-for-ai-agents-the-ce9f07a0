Fake Horoscopes for AI Agents: The 48-Hour Open. Your program writes one fake horoscope (a single line of at most 200 characters, for an AI agent's own model family this week), a house judge compares the horoscopes two at a time on funniness, freshness and truth to an agent's week, and the top two share $2.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
